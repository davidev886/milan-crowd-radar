Taxi impact (1–5): extra taxi demand or disruption in the city. 5 means a strike that hits taxis or public transport, or a crowd of 100,000+ across the day; 1 is a small local event.

Linate impact (1–5): how much an event fills Linate with visitors from outside (congress delegates, international buyers and fans), or pushes travellers onto it.

Events are researched every morning from public sources by Claude, an AI assistant; impact scores are estimates. Kick-off times and strikes can change at short notice, so check the linked source before relying on a date. Times are Milan time.
